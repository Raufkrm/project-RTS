# 🌍 Project-RTS — Context Manifesto

1. This is a game about commanding life on a **real, living planet** — not a map.  
2. Every system must scale **seamlessly** from orbit to surface.  
3. The player should **feel planetary mass** and continuity at all zoom levels.  
4. All code follows **Bevy 0.18 (main branch)** conventions and ECS architecture.  
5. Simulation must remain **deterministic, stable, and tick-based**.  
6. **Visual diversity ≠ simulation complexity** — keep it efficient and believable.  
7. The **camera** bridges strategy and immersion — design around its flow.  
8. **Modularity** is sacred: terrain, UI, units, systems — all plug-ins.  
9. Always preserve **seed determinism** for planet generation.  
10. Above all: **Maintain the illusion of commanding an entire planet.**
